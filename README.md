# Mark 3:28–30 — forgiveness, interpretation, and the human transmission of teaching

A writing and research workspace directed by Aaron Solomon.

The project investigates the tension between the breadth of forgiveness in Mark 3:28 and the warning in 3:29. Its questions concern semantic history, Gospel interpretation, Paul’s transformation, and the humanity of teachers and interpreters. Buddhist and Plum Village teaching is a comparative resource, not a substitute for Greek exegesis.

## Editorial status

This is a research workspace, not a finished article. Working prose and source notes are assistant-assisted unless a file states otherwise. Earlier drafts are preserved as historical drafts, not as endorsed statements or verified evidence.

The repository is public. Do not add identifying details about third parties or new autobiographical particulars without Aaron’s explicit approval. Community and third-party names have been omitted from the initial archive where appropriate; omissions are identified, not silently presented as verbatim text.

No full copyrighted books, articles, recordings, or third-party PDFs are stored here. Source notes use links, brief excerpts, and attributed summaries. No publication license is assigned by this setup.
