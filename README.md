# Mark 3:28–30 — forgiveness, interpretation, and the human transmission of teaching

A writing and research workspace directed by Aaron Solomon.

The project investigates the tension between the breadth of forgiveness in Mark 3:28 and the warning in 3:29. Its questions concern semantic history, Gospel interpretation, Paul’s transformation, and the humanity of teachers and interpreters. Buddhist and Plum Village teachings participate in the inquiry as substantive comparative resources, not substitutes for Greek exegesis.
