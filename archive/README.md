# Conversation archive — development, drafts, and reusable language

This is a **selective editorial archive of the supplied conversation**, prepared on 2026-10-10. It preserves the main successive frames, substantial passages, source leads, and reasons for changes. It is **not a raw platform export or a complete verbatim transcript**. Repeated prose, conversational transitions, decorative elements, and duplicate links are omitted. Stage numbers indicate order, not verified calendar dates.

The aim is to retain the work without making the current essay inherit every earlier claim or rejected stylistic choice. Source claims in these files are not newly validated by being archived. Use the [claim audit](../notes/claim-audit.md) and [current source notes](../sources/README.md) before reusing them.

## Provenance and privacy

“Assistant excerpt” means language composed by the assistant in the conversation, even when written in the first person. It is not independent evidence of events in Aaron’s life. “Aaron excerpt” means words from the supplied user draft or direction. “Editorial summary” is a new summary, not a transcript quotation. Formatting and paragraph breaks may be normalized.

The initial personal opening elsewhere in the thread named a community. Identifying community and third-party names are not reproduced in these public archive selections. No new incidents, motives, diagnoses, or details about affected people have been added. The statement that Aaron caused harm in 2021 was supplied by Aaron and appears here only in the form used in his own unnamed-community draft or clearly labeled assistant prose.

The current project does not imitate Thích Nhất Hạnh’s style. Earlier attempts at a contemplative style are retained as abandoned drafts, not templates governing future writing.

## What remains outside this archive

The complete raw conversation, every repeated revision, and all exact earlier citation renderings are not preserved. Some long drafts are represented by selected paragraphs plus section maps. A later user-provided conversation export can be added as a separate, privacy-reviewed source; this deposit should never be relabeled as that export.
