## Reading the status labels

- **Text checked:** the relevant written passage was inspected in the linked online edition. This does not claim a critical edition in the original language was consulted.
- **Excerpt checked:** a specified excerpt, rather than the entire work, was inspected.
- **Abstract/bibliography checked:** the article’s landing page was read; the full argument has not been assessed.
- **Catalog checked:** title/author/description or selected-edition details were confirmed. This is not a claim to have read the book.
- **Recording description checked:** the hosting institution’s summary was inspected, not the audio. Quote the summary as editorial text, not as the teacher’s exact speech.
- **Lead only:** retained from the conversation or a bibliography; locate and verify before using as evidence.

Source notes separate what a text says from what the project proposes to infer. When a source supports both a hopeful and a severe interpretation, retain both. Source IDs remain stable even if files grow.

## Quotation and translation conventions

Brief direct quotations identify their author and locator. English patristic quotations are translations, not the Greek or Latin originals. Biblical English supplied as a study rendering is our own unless an edition is named. Important Greek is accompanied by romanization, a segmented literal gloss, and ordinary English alternatives. A mechanical gloss is not automatically the best translation.

Links to online Bible displays are convenient access points, not substitutes for a named critical text and apparatus. Some sites display an English rendering that does not match the Greek noun exactly; check the Greek independently. Full original-language and manuscript work remains in the research queue.

## What has not been established

No full historical semantic trajectory has yet been demonstrated. No reconstruction of Jesus’s precise utterance is adopted. No universalist or irreversible-hardening conclusion is treated as settled. The interbeing of time is a declared comparative resource, not an etymology of aiōn. Rigidity is an interpretive proposal, not a new translation of Mark 3:29. See the [claim audit](../notes/claim-audit.md).
