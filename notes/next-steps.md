The initial repository is a starting research environment, not a completed source review.

## First research pass

1. Read James Barr on semantics and time, Gary Anderson on sin’s metaphors, and one current critical Mark commentary. Record page-specific notes and counterexamples before writing a historical trajectory.
2. Obtain and read the full Kręcidło article. Its abstract and bibliography were inspected; full-text retrieval did not succeed in this setup. Verify printed pagination against the journal’s metadata.
3. Check Mark 3:29 in NA28/UBS5 or ECM Mark. Record manuscript witnesses for “sin,” “judgment,” and other variants; do not infer scribal motives from variants alone.
4. Build a small Hebrew/Septuagint/Greek corpus for sin, remission, liability, and age-language. Include mercy and consequences together, not only evidence for a preferred reading.
5. Read Chrysostom’s entire argument in Homily 41 §5, Augustine Sermon 71 §§20–22, Aquinas II–II q.14 a.3, and both relevant MacDonald sermons. Source notes currently verify selected passages, not original-language critical editions.
6. Locate an exact TNH treatment of the interbeing of the three times in a checked transcript or recording. Sister Annabel’s direct discussion is already available. Do not turn an editorial talk summary into a quotation.

## Maintain a small repository

Add notes to existing topic files when practical. Archive substantive milestones, not every edit. Use Git diffs for routine revision. Store links and brief excerpts rather than full books or recordings. Do not add a site generator or citation-management dependency until there is a concrete need.
